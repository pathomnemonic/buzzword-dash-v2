## Rules for every post

- **Answers and reveals are always the NEXT day,** on every platform. Shorts and Reels end with "Answer tomorrow", never the answer.
- **On-image line, every post, every platform:** "Original educational teaching vignette — not an actual exam question."
- **Caption footer, every platform:** "Not medical advice. Original educational teaching vignette — not an actual exam question."
- Clue order: clue 1 hooks but never names the diagnosis, the last clue clinches. One separating feature per duel.
- Never claim these are real exam questions, never name NBME, UWorld, First Aid or AMBOSS, no pass-rate or score promises.
