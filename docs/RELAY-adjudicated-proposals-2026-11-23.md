## How the adjudication worked
- ACCEPT = proposal is factually right per its cited source and current guidelines AND right for a Step-style teaching game. Apply as written in the JSON.
- PARTIAL = direction is right; the adjustment below is binding unless you judge otherwise at application.
- Where boards still test an older standard, PARTIAL dual-labels it rather than replacing it outright.
- Gemini ran only its free tier — its blanket ACCEPTs were treated as weak-positive evidence. ChatGPT's substantive factual qualifications were generally adopted. Where a panelist misread (one case, P020), I corrected it from the proposal text itself.
- Contested facts were verified against primary sources before deciding where the models disagreed on a fact.
