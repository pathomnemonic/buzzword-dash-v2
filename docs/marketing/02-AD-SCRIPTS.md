# Ad scripts (24)

Format for every script: **Length · Platform · Hook (0 to 3 s) · Shots · On-screen text · Audio · CTA · Variants / test notes.** Vertical 9:16 unless stated. "Gameplay" means a screen recording of a real run (see 04-PRODUCTION-PLAN.md for capture specs). All captions are burned in (most viewers watch muted). Every video ends on the same end card: **DX DASH logo · "Free to start" · store badges · "Link in bio" (organic) / button (paid).**

Series key: **A** Scroll less (digital wellbeing, Finch-style warmth) · **B** Brain-rot friendly (meme voice) · **C** ADHD-friendly design (respectful, compliant) · **D** Straight gameplay · **E** Med-school humour and social.

Rules that apply to all: no claim to guarantee passing; no "cure/treat"; no "do you have X" lines; USMLE and COMLEX are named only to say what the questions are *like* ("board-style"); real UI only; if a creator says the line, they say it in their own words (the line here is a brief, not a teleprompter).

## Series A: Scroll less, learn more (digital wellbeing)

Tone: soft, honest, a little tired, never preachy. Think a friend with a cup of tea. Music: gentle lo-fi, then the game's chiptune on the cut.

**A1. "The 45-minute scroll"** · 15 s · TikTok / Reels / Shorts
- Hook: black screen, phone-screen glow, caption "It's 11:40pm. You meant to do one block."
- Shots: (1) thumb scrolling endless clips (stock-free: a creator's real feed blurred), timer overlay 0:45:12; (2) cut to Dx Dash gameplay, same thumb swipe lane to lane; (3) answer gate "Subarachnoid hemorrhage" hit, green flash, +coins; (4) streak counter 3.
- Text: "Same thumb." → "Different 5 minutes." → "Board-style questions, as a game."
- Audio: lo-fi hum, then a soft "ding" on the correct gate.
- CTA: "Swap one scroll. Free to start."
- Variants: swap hook text ("It's 11:40pm. You meant to do one block." / "You've been scrolling since the last lecture." / "Your screen time report is… a lot.") Test all three.

**A2. "Screen-time report"** · 12 s
- Hook: phone showing a Screen Time weekly report with a red "+34%" (made-up, a mock; label "example").
- Shots: report → thumb opens Dx Dash → quick run → "You answered 18 questions in 6 minutes" results card.
- Text: "You're going to be on your phone anyway." → "Make some of it count."
- CTA: "Dx Dash. Study that moves."
- Notes: the mock report must be clearly an example; no real person's data.

**A3. "Five-minute reset"** · 20 s
- Hook: creator on a couch, hands in a blanket: "I don't have it in me to open UWorld right now."
- Shots: creator → "so I did a run instead" → gameplay montage (3 gates, a power-up, the monster behind) → creator: "I did 20 questions without feeling like I was studying."
- Text: "Low-energy study."  "No guilt required."
- Audio: creator voice + soft music.
- CTA: "It's free. Try one run."
- Notes: creator says it in their words; do not claim it helps any condition.

**A4. "Streak, not shame"** · 15 s
- Hook: close-up of a flame streak "12 days".
- Shots: streak → missing a day → a shield icon saves it ("streak shield") → "welcome back" screen with no scolding.
- Text: "A streak that forgives you." "(Because you're a person.)"
- CTA: "Build a habit that's kind."
- Notes: confirm the shield mechanic matches the build (streak shields exist: earned every few streak days).

**A5. "Make the doomscroll useful"** · 10 s (Reels cut)
- Hook: kinetic text "doomscrolling →" crossed out → "doom-studying (with a monster chasing you)".
- Shots: 3 fast gameplay clips.
- Audio: trending-style bass hit (use a licensed/royalty-free track).
- CTA: "Free. 3,000+ questions."

**A6. "Study with me (but it's a game)"** · 30 s
- Hook: "Study with me, but I'm actually having fun."
- Shots: a creator's full 2-minute run sped up; overlays of correct answers and the leaderboard moving up; ending on the day's goal bar filling.
- Text: running tally "Cards today: 7, 14, 20 ✓".
- CTA: "Today's goal: 20 cards."

## Series B: Brain-rot friendly (the meme voice)

Tone: chaotic on the surface, genuinely rigorous underneath. One joke per video; the clinical content on screen is always real and correct. Use trending *formats* (not copyrighted audio unless licensed through the platform's commercial music library). Keep it affectionate, never mean.

**B1. "Brain rot friendly studying"** · 12 s
- Hook: fast-cut text "me: I'll study" / "also me: 🧠🍟 (brain rot)".
- Shots: flash of meme-style sticker overlays on real gameplay; gate answer pops in big pixel text "CORRECT 💥".
- Text: "brain rot friendly studying" (as the title card) → "it still teaches real vignettes" (small, honest).
- Audio: a trending commercial-library sound.
- CTA: "Rot your brain productively."

**B2. "Skibidi-level reflexes, board-level knowledge"** · 10 s
- Hook: a deliberately absurd caption over a hero running: "no thoughts, only dodge".
- Shots: dodge obstacle → "Pheochromocytoma" gate → big text "ALPHA-BLOCKER FIRST" popping like a meme caption.
- Text: "no thoughts head empty" → "(the thoughts are in the question bank)".
- Notes: keep slang light and current; refresh every month (stale slang kills it); avoid targeting any group.

**B3. "POV: the monster is your Step date"** · 15 s
- Hook: POV text "POV: your exam date is the monster."
- Shots: the in-game monster closing in as lives drop; the player answers 3 in a row; the monster falls back.
- Text: "run. answer. survive."
- CTA: "Outrun the monster. Free."

**B4. "NPC study routine"** · 12 s
- Hook: robotic NPC voice over gameplay: "Daily goal complete. Streak maintained. Coins collected."
- Shots: HUD close-ups (coins, streak, goal), the daily reward chest.
- Text: "NPC energy, real results" (avoid "results" if it implies a promised score; use "NPC energy, actual flashcards").
- CTA: "Be the NPC who passes."  Compliance: "passes" is aspirational but not a guarantee; if flagged, use "Be the NPC who studied."

**B5. "Rizz the leaderboard"** · 15 s
- Hook: "me beating my co-resident's score at 2am".
- Shots: leaderboard climb; friend request; kudos emoji; "Beat my score" challenge link tapped.
- Text: "friendly rivalry arc".
- CTA: "Send them a challenge."

**B6. "Rate my hero"** · 12 s (trend-friendly, interactive)
- Hook: "Rate my hero 1 to 10, then pick my next answer in the comments."
- Shots: Locker hero lineup, then a question where comments decide the gate.
- CTA: "Pick the answer. I'll run it."
- Notes: pair with a reply-to-comments follow-up (creator replies in video). Great for engagement; use real comment answers.

## Series C: ADHD-friendly design (compliant, respectful)

Hard rule: talk about **how the app is built**, not about the viewer. No "Do you have ADHD?", no "if you struggle with focus…", no diagnosis language, no medical claims, no claim that the app helps symptoms. The phrase to use is **"designed with ADHD-friendly features"** or "ADHD-friendly design", plus concrete features. Where a creator has ADHD and wants to share their experience, it is **their** statement in first person ("as someone with ADHD, I like that…"), labelled as their opinion, and the ad is run through the creator's page or with the creator's consent. Do not run Series C with targeting on health or interest segments that infer a condition.

Concrete features to name: short sessions (a run is 3 to 8 minutes), instant feedback on every answer, small frequent rewards (coins, streaks, chest), visual progress (daily goal bar), no timers that punish, **streak shields**, reduced motion mode, dyslexia-friendly font, colorblind-safe colors, left-hand layout, no ads, works offline, no required reading before you start.

**C1. "Designed with ADHD-friendly features"** · 15 s
- Hook: text "A study app with ADHD-friendly design."
- Shots: five 2-second feature beats with captions: "3-minute runs" · "instant feedback" · "rewards every answer" · "streak shields" · "reduced motion mode".
- CTA: "Free to try."

**C2. Creator: "As someone with ADHD…"** · 25 s (creator-owned, first person)
- Hook (creator's words): "As someone with ADHD, here's why I actually open this one."
- Shots: creator talking → gameplay B-roll while they point at features.
- Disclosure: "#ad / Paid partnership" on screen and in caption; creator may say what works for them; **no claims it treats or helps ADHD**.
- Notes: brief the creator with 07-COMPLIANCE.md; they approve the final cut.

**C3. "Why a run is short on purpose"** · 15 s
- Hook: "Why every run is under 8 minutes."
- Shots: timeline graphic: start → 5 questions → reward → quit anytime (no penalty) → streak shield.
- Text: "Start fast. Stop anytime. Nothing lost."
- CTA: "Try one run."

**C4. "No ads. No walls of text."** · 12 s
- Hook: split screen: a cluttered app vs Dx Dash's clean Home.
- Text: "No ads. No popups mid-run. No 200-word questions to read before you can start." (accurate: questions are buzzword-style vignettes; tutorial is short)
- CTA: "Study without the clutter."

**C5. "Accessibility is a feature, not a setting buried in menus"** · 20 s
- Hook: "Dyslexia font. Colorblind-safe. Reduced motion. Left-handed layout."
- Shots: flip each toggle in Settings with the instant UI change.
- Text: "Built so more brains can use it."
- CTA: "Settings → Look. Try them."
- Notes: this one can run broadly; it is an accessibility message, not a health claim.

## Series D: Straight gameplay (the workhorse ads)

Tone: confident and clean. 100% real capture. These are the control group for every test.

**D1. "Run. Answer. Repeat."** · 15 s: 3 gates back-to-back, caption the diagnosis each time. CTA "Free to start."
**D2. "Wrong answer = the monster gets closer"** · 12 s: miss → monster closes in → recover → save. Text: "Mistakes have stakes."
**D3. "Every miss teaches you"** · 15 s: miss → "why" explanation card → next run asks it again later (spaced repetition). Text: "It remembers what you forgot." Claim check: FSRS scheduling is real.
**D4. "300 questions free. 3,010 with Pro."** · 15 s: free library counter → paywall → unlock animation. Text: "Start free. Unlock the whole bank when you're ready." CTA to install; this is the only ad that mentions Pro.
