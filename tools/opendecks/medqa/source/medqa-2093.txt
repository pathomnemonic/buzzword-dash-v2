A 27-year-old male is brought to the emergency room following a violent assault in which he was stabbed in the chest. The knife penetrated both the left lung and the left ventricle. Where did the knife most likely enter his chest?
Options: A) Left seventh intercostal space in the midaxillary line; B) Left seventh intercostal space in the midclavicular line; C) Left fifth intercostal space in the midaxillary line; D) Left fifth intercostal space in the midclavicular line.
The keyed answer is D) Left fifth intercostal space in the midclavicular line.
