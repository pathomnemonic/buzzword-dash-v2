Alpo syndrome: EM is diagnostic and shows "basket weave appearance" of the GBM. Alpo syndrome: Hereditary nephritis Abnormal a3 (COL4A3), a4 (COL4A4), or a5 (COL4A5) chain of type IV collagen Hematuria with progression to chronic renal failure Nerve deafness Eye disorders Foamy cells in the interstitium.
