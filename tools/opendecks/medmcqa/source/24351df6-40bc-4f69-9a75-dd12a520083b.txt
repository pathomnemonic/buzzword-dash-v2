Nonessential amino acids—can be synthesized in humans. a. Alanine—transamination from pyruvate. b. Aspartate—transamination from oxaloacetate. c. Asparagine—addition of ammonia to aspartate. d. Cysteine—skeleton from serine and sulfur from methionine. e. Glutamate—transamination from α-ketoglutarate. f. Glutamine—addition of ammonia to glutamate.
