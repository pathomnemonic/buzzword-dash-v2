Flavoproteins & Iron-Sulfur proteins (Fe-S) are Components of the Respiratory Chain Complexes.Iron-sulfur proteins (nonheme iron proteins, Fe-S) are found in Complexes I, II, and III. These may contain one, two, or four Fe atoms linked to inorganic sulfur atoms and/or cysteine SH groups to the protein.
