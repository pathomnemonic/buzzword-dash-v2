Placental abruption can lead to initiation of the coagulation cascade by release of tissue thromboplastin into the maternal circulation. Consumption of coagulation factors and platelets is followed by coagulopathic hemorrhage. A cycle ensues as fuher bleeding worsens the depletion of coagulation factors.
