3. OpenStax archive JSON of the book (used as the text source): "license": {"url": "http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/", "name": "Creative Commons Attribution License"}.

4. The web reader footer of https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology/pages/1-introduction says: "Textbook content produced by OpenStax
   is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution License." (One templated "Attribution information" box on that page still mentions
   CC-BY-NC-SA; the PDF, the CMS record, the archive metadata and the footer all say CC BY 4.0. Flagged as an open point in docs/OPEN-DECKS.md.)

NOT used: Anatomy and Physiology 2e (the current edition) is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 per its CMS record and web page, so it is excluded. In fact
every OpenStax book that is still "live" except Physics and Introduction to Intellectual Property is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 now, including
Microbiology, Biology 2e, Pharmacology for Nurses and Medical-Surgical Nursing; only the retired earlier editions remain CC BY 4.0.
