Privacy Policy

Dx Dash · Last updated 6 October 2026

Dx Dash is a study game for medical board exams. It works fully without an account, and it has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking. If you agree, it shares anonymous usage statistics with the developer (see "Anonymous usage statistics" below). This page explains exactly what is stored and where.

Stored on your device

Your progress (scores, streaks, coins, unlocked items, per-question history), settings, and any custom cards you create are saved on your device. If you never turn on online features, none of this leaves your device. You can erase it by clearing the app's data, using Settings, or uninstalling the app.

Online features (optional)

Leaderboards, friends, groups, activity feed, weekly tournaments and cloud saves use a hosted database (Supabase). When you use them we store:

  • An account identifier. Everyone who uses online features gets one, even as a guest, so scores and friends can be tied to you.
  • Your email address and a hashed password, only if you create an account. The email is used for signing in, confirming your address and resetting your password. We do not send marketing email.
  • Your display name, avatar, badges and scores (best score, streak, mode, speed), and a record of friend requests, group membership and activity items you generate (for example a finished run with its score and accuracy, a personal best, or a study streak). Each activity item is shared with your friends or kept private to you, as you choose in Friends → Feed, and you can hide or delete any item afterwards. Friends can give you kudos on items they can see.
  • A cloud copy of your progress, if you are signed in with an account, so it follows you across devices. It is visible only to you.
  • Reports you send about a question or a player.

Your display name, avatar, badges and scores can be seen by other players on leaderboards, and by your friends and group members. You can hide your profile from search in the leaderboard screen.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional, you are asked once)

The first time you open the app it asks whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics. If you say yes (or turn it on later in Settings → Data), the app sends the developer's own database (Supabase; no analytics company is involved) a record of how it is used so the game can be improved:

  • How you play: which modes you pick, how long a run lasts, how many questions you answer and how fast, your score, power-ups used and how runs end.
  • Which questions were shown and whether they were answered correctly, so weak or confusing questions can be fixed. This is tied to the question, not to what you typed.
  • What you open and use in the app: screens, the Locker, purchases with in-game coins, quests, settings and accessibility options you use, and whether you finish the tutorial.
  • Your device in broad terms: phone, tablet or computer, operating system and browser family, a rounded screen size, language, time-zone offset, app version, connection type, graphics level and how smoothly the game ran.
  • Problems: crashes and errors, with a short message and where they happened.
  • Where you found the app: campaign tags in the link you opened (such as utm_source), the website that sent you (its host name only), and whether an ad-click identifier was present (the identifier itself is not kept). Links the app creates for sharing carry a short code, a one-way hash, so the developer can count how many installs a share brought in.

All of it is tied to a random identifier generated on your device. It is not tied to your name, email address, account, contacts, location or any advertising or device identifier, and it is never sold or shared with advertisers. Anything you type (custom cards, notes, feedback text) is never part of it. The app keeps no cookies for this; the identifier lives in the app's local storage.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, usage statistics stay off unless you turn them on yourself. You can switch them off at any time in Settings → Data, which also deletes what the app has queued. Settings → Data → Delete my analytics data removes everything already sent for this device from the server and gives the device a new random identifier. Statistics are kept for at most 26 months, then deleted automatically. The app also counts, without any identifier, how many people said yes or no to the question.

Tips (optional)

In the phone apps you can leave a tip through the Google Play or Apple App Store payment system. Payment is handled entirely by the store: we never see your name, card or payment details. A tip unlocks nothing. If you have agreed to anonymous usage statistics, they record only that a tip was started, completed or cancelled and its price, tied to the random identifier described above. On the website, the tip link opens an outside payment page with its own privacy policy.

Crash and performance reports (optional, off by default)

In Settings → Data you can switch on "Help fix problems". When it is on, the game sends an anonymous report if it crashes or runs slowly: a short error message, the part of the game it happened in, the app version and your graphics level. No account identifier, name, scores, cards or device identifiers are stored with it. Reports are only used to fix bugs.

Feedback you choose to send

If you tap "Not really" when asked whether you enjoy the game, or use Settings → About & help → Send feedback, the message you write is sent to the developer along with the app version and the kind of device (Android, iPhone or web), linked to your player account so we can answer you. If you add an email address it is only used to reply. If the message cannot be sent from the app it may be sent by an email you review first. Feedback is only used to improve the game and is deleted when you delete your account.

Multiplayer

Live multiplayer connects players directly to each other (peer to peer) using PeerJS. A public PeerJS server helps the two devices find each other, and the player you connect to can see your network address and display name while you are connected.

Who processes your data

Supabase hosts the database and sign-in. PeerJS provides the connection-setup service for multiplayer. Your web host (for the website version) may keep ordinary server logs. We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all online data (profile, scores, friends, groups and cloud save) at any time from Leaderboard → Account → Delete my account. Guests can use Delete my online data in the same place. Progress stored only on your device is not affected; clear it through your device or Settings.

Children

Dx Dash is intended for medical students and other adults and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed by Supabase, and database rules limit each account to its own private data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions or deletion requests: please open an issue at github.com/pathomnemonic/buzzword-dash-v2/issues.
