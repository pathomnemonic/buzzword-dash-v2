Delete your Dx Dash account and data

Dx Dash · Last updated 2 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Dx Dash and tap Friends on the Home screen.
  2. Open the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account (or Delete my online data if you never made an account) and confirm.

This deletes immediately and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in, display name and picture, scores, friend requests and friendships, group memberships and any groups left empty, the activity items you posted and the kudos you gave or received, any study-buddy listing, and your cloud save. Reports you sent about others are removed with your account.

What is not affected

Progress stored only on your device (your saved game, settings and custom cards). Clear it through your device's app settings or by uninstalling the app. Anonymous crash reports, if you chose to send them, are not linked to you and cannot be traced back to delete.

Cannot open the app?

Open an issue at github.com/pathomnemonic/buzzword-dash-v2/issues asking for deletion, with the display name or email you used, and we will delete the account.
