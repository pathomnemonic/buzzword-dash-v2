Delete your Dx Dash account and data

Dx Dash · Last updated 2 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Dx Dash and tap Friends on the Home screen.
  2. Open the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete my Dx Dash data to remove everything Dx Dash holds about you, or Delete my login everywhere to also remove your sign-in itself (see below). If you never made an account, tap Delete my online data.
  4. Confirm. For "Delete my login everywhere" you will also be asked to type DELETE.

This deletes immediately and cannot be undone.

Two choices, because your sign-in may be shared

The same sign-in (your email, Google, Apple or Microsoft login) can be used in other apps by the same developer. Delete my Dx Dash data removes only what Dx Dash holds and leaves your sign-in in place, so you can still use your other apps. Delete my login everywhere removes your Dx Dash data and then your sign-in itself, which removes you from every app that uses it. Either way, a subscription that is still billing is cancelled first, and if it cannot be, nothing is deleted.

What is deleted

With either choice: your display name and picture, scores, friend requests and friendships, group memberships and any groups left empty, the activity items you posted and the kudos you gave or received, any study-buddy listing, your cloud save, and your Pro and purchase records. Reports you sent about others are removed too. With Delete my login everywhere, your sign-in (email address and any Google, Apple or Microsoft link) is deleted as well.

What is not affected

Progress stored only on your device (your saved game, settings and custom cards). Clear it through your device's app settings or by uninstalling the app. Anonymous crash reports, if you chose to send them, are not linked to you and cannot be traced back to delete.

Cannot open the app?

Email patho.mnemonic1@gmail.com asking for deletion, from the address you signed up with (or tell us the display name you used), and we will delete the account.
